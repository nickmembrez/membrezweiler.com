Born Lucky

Nobody chooses where they start.
Built for SOC 1113. The simulation on this page is an illustration, not evidence. Its rules were written by hand to reflect patterns described in the research below, and none of its numbers were estimated from data. Do not cite it. The measured figures come from Chetty, Hendren, Kline, and Saez 2014 (income mobility and the quintile transition matrix, tax records of about 40 million families) and the Pell Institute, Indicators of Higher Education Equity in the United States, 2024 (bachelor’s attainment by family income quarter). The model's design draws on Annette Lareau, Unequal Childhoods (parenting and class), Pierre Bourdieu (cultural and social capital), and Oliver and Shapiro, Black Wealth/White Wealth (income vs wealth). All people and places in the simulation are fictional.
