Survey Questions Written by an AI Tool SOCL 3633 Research Methods · Lab 7 · Midwestern State University

AI tools will write survey questions on any topic in a few seconds. Are the questions any good? Today a free AI tool drafts ten survey questions on the concept from your research question. You find the problems in each question and repair the three worst. Your grade is for the quality of your diagnosis and repairs, not for what the tool wrote. Work through the tabs in order. Before you leave, you paste your response sheet into the Lab 7 submission on D2L.

Repair the three worst questions

        
Choose the three questions with the most serious problems for measuring your concept. Rewrite each one with its answer choices. Your repaired questions are candidates for questions 4 to 10 of your Lab 8 survey.

        
Most repairs make a question more specific: one topic per question, a clear and recent time span, plain words, answer choices that do not overlap and fit everyone, and wording that asks about your concept directly.
