The MovementSOC 1113 · How Social Change Actually Happens

A fictional game built for SOC 1113, Introduction to Sociology, Midwestern State University. The 3.5% figure and the finding that nonviolent campaigns succeed about twice as often as violent ones come from Erica Chenoweth and Maria Stephan, Why Civil Resistance Works (2011), and Chenoweth's later research on over 300 campaigns since 1900.
