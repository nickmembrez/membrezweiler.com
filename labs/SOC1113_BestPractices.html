A fictional game built for SOC 1113, Introduction to Sociology, Midwestern State University. Ideas in play: Max Weber on rationalization and the iron cage. George Ritzer, The McDonaldization of Society, including the irrationality of rationality. Arlie Hochschild, The Managed Heart, on emotional labor. Harry Braverman on deskilling. Antonio Gramsci on hegemony and Michael Burawoy on manufactured consent.
